Research

My research interests involve multimodal large language models (MLLM / VLM) and 3D vision.
I am currently interested in VLM post-training and agentic RL.
I have previously conducted some work in 2D/3D scene understanding, especially in scene parsing and 3D Gaussian splatting.

2026.10: I am looking for collaboration / internship opportunities related to VLM or agent. Feel free to contact me!
